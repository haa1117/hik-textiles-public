# Technical Case Study

## Brief

Give a yarn manufacturer a credible, fast, mobile-friendly web presence that explains the business and turns visitors into enquiries, without adding a backend to maintain.

## Approach

1. **Single-page React app** with five routes, so navigation is instant after the first load.
2. **Tailwind CSS** for a consistent theme of muted sage and cream tones with Belleza headings and Poppins body text.
3. **Photography-led layout** using real mill and product imagery, a looping video hero, and decorative line art.
4. **Static delivery.** The production build is plain files on a web host.
5. **Form via hosted email service** with client-side validation and clear feedback.

## What was built

- Five pages sharing a navbar and footer
- A looping inline video hero
- Visibility-triggered section animation and count-up figures
- Four product-line presentations and an industries-served list
- A validated contact form
- Responsive behaviour from large desktop down to phone width

## What this showcase does not claim

- No traffic, conversion or enquiry figures are available or implied.
- No search ranking, Lighthouse or accessibility-audit results are claimed.
- The company's statements about its production, certifications and impact are the company's own.

## Possible next steps

- Poster image and a smaller video encode for faster first paint
- Per-page titles and descriptions, and a canonical URL in the document head
- A shared hook for the visibility observers
- Content kept in data files so copy updates do not touch layout code
