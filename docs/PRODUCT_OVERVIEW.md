# Product Overview

## Purpose

HIK Textiles manufactures synthetic and blended yarns. The website introduces the company, explains what it makes and for whom, and gives prospective buyers a way to get in touch. The live site is [hiktextiles.com](https://hiktextiles.com/).

## Pages

| Route | Purpose | Content |
|---|---|---|
| `/` | Introduction | Video hero, Who We Are, four product-line cards, an impact section with counters, certification badges, a closing image band |
| `/about` | Company story | A timeline-style page: founding in 2018, certifications, resilience through the pandemic, community purpose, and four company values |
| `/services` | Capabilities | Three service areas (protective and performance yarns, specialty and custom blends, sustainable and recycled yarns) and five industries served |
| `/products` | Catalogue overview | Four product lines with a short description and image each |
| `/get-in-touch` | Enquiries | Contact form, plus address and contact details in the footer |

## Visitor journeys

1. **Evaluate the company.** Home, then About for background and certifications.
2. **Find a yarn.** Home product cards or Products, then Services for end-use industries.
3. **Make contact.** Any page's "Get in Touch" button leads to the form.

## Content ownership

Company claims on the site (founding year, certifications, figures in the impact section) are statements by HIK Textiles. This portfolio describes how the site presents them; it does not independently verify them.
