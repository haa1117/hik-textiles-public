# Privacy and Source

## What this repository is

A portfolio showcase for a production company website. It describes the engineering of the HIK Textiles site; it is not a source distribution.

## What is published

- Written descriptions of structure, design decisions and trade-offs
- Original diagrams created for this showcase
- Real screenshots of the public website, desktop and mobile

## What is intentionally not published

- Production source code, in whole or in part
- Build, hosting and deployment configuration
- Environment files and the identifiers used by the form service
- The company's raw photography, video and brand asset files
- Version control history of the production repository
- Any visitor or enquiry data
- Company contact details beyond what appears in the screenshots

## How the descriptions were produced

The site's source was reviewed locally and the live site at hiktextiles.com was loaded in a browser. Statements describe behaviour observed in code or on the live page. Company marketing statements are attributed to the company and are not presented as independently verified.

## Images

Screenshots are captures of the public site. No form was submitted and no personal data appears in them. Images were re-encoded as WebP with metadata removed.

## Publication checks

Before publishing, every file was checked for source code, credentials, personal data, local file-system paths and previous account references. None remain.

## Access

Source access for hiring or due-diligence purposes can be discussed at the owner's discretion.
